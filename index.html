Puzzle No. 1

Tethered

Tie up the loose ends

A new daily word puzzle from Puzzl

How to play

Sixteen tags hide four long words, each chopped into four pieces. Tap the four pieces of one word, then press Tie them.

The strip under the tags shows what your pieces spell in the order you tap them. You can tie them in any order, though.

Watch out: some pieces join up into words that aren't the answer. Only one way of using all sixteen works.

You can make four mistakes. If you're down to your last one, you can ask for a hint.

Every puzzle has a loose theme. You'll find out what it was when you finish.

Everyone gets the same puzzle. A new one arrives every day at 7am UK time.
