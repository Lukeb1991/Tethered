Tethered

Tie up the loose ends

A new daily connections puzzle from Puzzl Lab

How to play

Sixteen tags hide four groups of four. Tap four tags you think share a link, then press Tie them.

Each group has a colour, from the most obvious to the trickiest:

EasiestTrickiest

Watch out for tags that look like they fit more than one group. Only one way of sorting them works.

You can make four mistakes. If you're down to your last one, you can ask for a hint.

Everyone gets the same puzzle. A new one arrives every day at 7am UK time.
